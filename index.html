Meet your guide · 你的向导

你好，我是 Joey

A friend who happens
to know every alley.

I was born in Beijing, raised between the hutongs and the high-rises. After a decade walking travelers from more than thirty countries through China, I learned one simple thing — the best moments aren't on any guidebook page.

So I started ORIENTTRIP to do it on my own terms — small groups, no tour buses, no rushed checklists. Just the China I would show a friend visiting from abroad. Tea with my old calligraphy teacher in Liulichang. Late-night lamb skewers in a hutong nobody photographs. The Great Wall at dawn, alone.
